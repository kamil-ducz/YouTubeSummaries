**Lord of the Rings – Quick‑and‑Casual Recap**

- **Shire & Bilbo** – Hobbits love food, not adventure. Bilbo’s birthday party = prank + disappears with the *One Ring* (invisibility, hard to give up).  
- **Ring’s History** – Sauron forged the master ring; a human king cut it off his finger, died, and the ring was lost for ages.  
- **Gollum & Bilbo** – Gollum finds the ring, later Bilbo “borrows” it in *The Hobbit*.  

### Fellowship Begins
- **Frodo gets the ring** → Gandalf says it’s too corrupting for him, so Frodo (incorruptible hobbit) must take it.  
- **Sam, Merry & Pippin** join; they’re mostly just walking.  
- **Black Riders (Nazgûl)** chase them; meet **Strider/Aragorn** (ranger, secret king).  
- **Arwen (Elf)** rescues Frodo from a Nazgûl, uses river magic.  

### Rivendell & Council
- **Gandalf vs. Saruman** – Saruman turned evil, traps Gandalf, but Gandalf escapes via the Eagles.  
- **Council of Elrond** decides the ring must be destroyed in Mount Doom.  
- **Fellowship formed:** Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli, Boromir, Gandalf.  

### Journey Through Moria
- **Moria** = dwarf tomb, Balrog awakens.  
- **Gandalf’s “You shall not pass!”** → falls with the Balrog.  

### Lothlórien & Breaking Up
- **Galadriel** gives Frodo a “phial of light.”  
- **Boromir tries to take the ring**, gets killed; the Fellowship splits.  

### Two Towers
- **Frodo & Sam** meet **Gollum** (Smeagol) → he leads them to secret pass into Mordor.  
- **Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli** rescue Merry & Pippin; meet **Treebeard** (Ent).  
- **Saruman’s army** attacks **Helm’s Deep** → Gandalf returns as **White**, defeats Saruman.  

### Return of the King
- **Rohan** (Theoden, Eowyn) fights Saruman’s forces; **King’s speech** + cavalry charge wins.  
- **Denethor** (steward of Gondor) goes crazy, dies; **Faramir** leads defense of Minas Tirith.  
- **Pippin lights the beacon**, Rohan rides to aid.  
- **Battle of the Pelennor Fields** – huge orc clash, **Eowyn + Merry** kill the Witch‑King.  

### The Final Push
- **Aragorn** leads a diversion at the Black Gate to buy Frodo time.  
- **Frodo & Sam** crawl through Mordor, face **Shelob** (giant spider). Sam saves Frodo.  
- **Mount Doom** – Frodo can’t let go, puts on the ring; **Gollum bites it off**, falls into lava → ring destroyed, Sauron’s tower collapses.  

### Epilogue
- **Aragorn crowned King**, marries Arwen.  
- **Hobbits return to the Shire**; Sam marries, has kids.  
- **Frodo, Bilbo, Elves** sail West (to the “Undying Lands”).  

*All that walking, fighting, and ring‑drama = Middle‑Earth saved.* 🌟
